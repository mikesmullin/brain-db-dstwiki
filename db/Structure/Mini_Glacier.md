It is fullest during Winter, melts through Spring into a puddle by Summer, and begins freezing again in Autumn. Heat sources such as Campfires melt it; a Campfire placed next to a melted glacier eliminates the puddle. When heat is removed, it can refreeze if ambient temperature is low enough, returning to the maximum size allowed by the temperature. Mining it makes a distinct sound from ordinary boulders. Lunar Hail can leave lunar glass embedded in it; Bright-Beaked Birds can remove the glass and drop a Moon Shard or Infused Moon Shard."
  renew: Yes; mined stages replenish in 1–3 days during Autumn or Winter. Rockyland glaciers regrow after Summer; Pengull-ground glaciers disappear in Summer. Ice Sheet glaciers do not melt in Summer.
