  description: "The DST Farm Plant system grows crops from Crop Seeds or generic Seeds planted in Farm Soil; unlike the older Basic and Improved Farm plots, crops develop through five stages and their accumulated stress determines yield."
  fields:
  - "Growth stages: Seeds, Sprout, Small, Medium, Grown. Conditions are checked at each stage; stress can reach 28. At 0–1 stress a plant yields a Giant Crop; at 2–6 it yields 1 crop and 2 seeds; at 7–11 it yields 1 crop and 1 seed; at 12+ it yields only 1 crop."
  - "Seven stress sources: insufficient nutrients, insufficient moisture, nearby weeds/rotten plants/Garden Detritus (within 1.5 tiles), off-season growth, fewer than 4 same-crop plants within 1 tile, more than 10 plants or weeds on one tile, and failure to tend at each stage."
  - "Plants grow only in daylight: surface Day and Dusk, or under Cave Lights, Lux Aeterna, a Dwarf Star, or Fire Ball. Growth is twice as fast in a crop's preferred seasons; random Seeds germinate in 0.75–1 day and have a 20% chance to produce a Weed."
  - "Growth is slower as stress rises. Total low-stress growth is about 2.5 daylight days in-season and 5 off-season; high-stress growth is about 4 and 8 daylight days. Wormwood-planted crops last 50% longer after maturity; normal crops rot in 4 days, giant crops in 6, and a rotted non-giant plant regrows after 4–5 days but cannot become giant."
  - A crop can be researched at every growth stage with a Gardeneer Hat; identification persists across worlds. The Plant Registry is opened by right-clicking the hat. Premier Gardeneer Hat reveals soil nutrient levels; Wormwood sees them naturally.
  - "At least 15 mature or rotten plants within 1 tile may summon the Lord of the Fruit Flies. Picking a non-giant rotten farm plant has a 5% chance to spawn a Fruit Fly. Farm Plants do not smolder by themselves in wildfires; Dragon Fruit is fireproof."
  - "Plant nutrient changes per growth stage (Growth Formula, Compost, Manure): Toma Root −2/−2/+4; Eggplant +2/+2/−4; Potato +2/+2/−4; Dragon Fruit +4/+4/−8; Pepper +4/+4/−8; Carrot −4/+2/+2; Pumpkin −4/+2/+2; Onion −8/+4/+4; Pomegranate −8/+4/+4; Asparagus +2/−4/+2; Corn +2/−4/+2; Durian +4/−8/+4; Garlic +4/−8/+4; Watermelon +4/−2/−2."
  - "Watering Can, Waterfowl Can, or melting Ice adds 25 moisture; Water Balloons add 20; rain adds up to 1.5 per second. Soil moisture ranges from 0 to 100 and decreases with temperature; more growing plants drain it. Watering once per stage is usually enough. A crop must spend at least 10% of each stage on wet ground to avoid moisture stress."
  - "A Birdcage bird fed a raw crop from Farm Soil produces 1–2 Crop Seeds of that crop and has a 50% chance to also produce one regular Seed; Berries and Mandrakes do not yield Crop Seeds, and birds will not eat Crop Seeds."
  - "Wormwood can plant anywhere without tilling and blooming Wormwood tends plants. Other characters use a Garden Digamajig to create Farm Soil and a Garden Hoe to till mounds. A Friendly Fruit Fly, Gramophone, One-man Band, blooming Wormwood, or WX-78's Chorusbox Circuit can tend crops; catching fire or being attacked by enemy Fruit Flies cancels tending."
  - Onion and Pomegranate consume 8 Growth Formula and produce 4 each Compost and Manure per stage; Durian and Garlic consume 8 Compost and produce 4 each other nutrient. Nutrient-balanced crop combinations can self-fertilize when the soil begins with enough nutrients.
