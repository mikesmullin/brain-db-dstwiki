  description: Four farm weeds interfere with Farm Plants but also provide materials and other benefits. Weeds can be removed with a Shovel or by burning them.
  fields:
  - "The four types are Forget-Me-Lots, Fire Nettles, Tillweed, and Spiny Bindweed. Planting a regular Seed has a 20% chance to grow a weed; conditional on growing a weed, 77% become Forget-Me-Lots and 7.7% each become one of the other types."
  - "At the beginning of Spring, each Farm Soil tile has a 15% chance to grow a weed; at the beginning of Autumn, each has a 5% chance. After reaching full growth, weeds spread nearby after 6–8 days (Forget-Me-Lots: 3–5 days), then attempt to spread every 12–14 days (Forget-Me-Lots: 6–8 days). Fruit Flies can plant weeds on unused Farm Soil within sight."
  - "Weed growth is not affected by season or nutrient levels. Each growth stage consumes 2 Growth Formula, 2 Compost, and 2 Manure, returning none. Fire Nettles, Tillweed, and Spiny Bindweed have three growth stages; Forget-Me-Lots has an additional Bolting stage. Forget-Me-Lots becomes unharvestable if it bolts."
  - "At full growth, Forget-Me-Lots, Fire Nettles, and Tillweed can be harvested and regrow from the start of their small stage. Dug Forget-Me-Lots may regrow nearby after 2–5 days, with chance falling from 75% when alone to 0% when at least three others are nearby; burning prevents regrowth."
  - Fire Nettles damage and overheat on contact unless wet; Wormwood is immune. Tillweed generates Garden Detritus. Spiny Bindweed protects nearby plants but traps harvesters with Binding Roots; Wormwood is immune to those roots.
