  description: "Dead creature bodies can persist or mutate under specific world conditions. Many eligible mobs have a 50% chance to leave a carcass that bursts into its mutated form after a few seconds when killed on Lunar Island, in Lunar Grotto, on a Lunar Rift Floor, during Moonstorm, under Lunar Hail, or during an Enlightenment Full Moon. Hounds killed by a Possessed Varg's frost breath always leave a carcass and become Horror Hounds. While Lunar Rifts are active, an Incursive Gestalt can possess Deerclops, Bearger, Varg, Buzzard, and bird carcasses and reanimate the dead mob. Burning a carcass prevents its mutation. Crystal-Crested Buzzards can eat carcasses; while one is nearby, expected mob loot is withheld, and eating alters or reduces the drops. Examples include Dead Hound → Horror Hound, Dead Pengull → Permafrost Pengull, Dead Merm → Mutated Merm, Dead Bearger → Armored Bearger, and Dead Varg → Possessed Varg. Clockworks, plant mobs, stone, clay, and cookie-made mobs do not leave carcasses; Rock Lobsters, Grass Gekkos, and Grass Gators can."
