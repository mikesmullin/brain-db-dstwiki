  description: "Food is obtained by foraging, farming, trapping, and hunting. It restores Hunger and may affect Health and Sanity; cooking, Crock Pot recipes, drying, character diet, freshness, storage, and temperature all change its value and use."
  fields:
  - "Freshness: fresh food (50–100%) gives full listed values; stale (21–49%) gives two-thirds Hunger and one-third Health, with no Sanity; spoiled (1–20%) gives half Hunger, no Health, and −10 Sanity. WX-78 ignores the stale and spoiled eating penalties."
  - "Cooking on a fire or in a Crock Pot halves the food's current spoilage. Cooked meat generally lasts longer than raw meat; raw vegetables generally last longer than cooked vegetables, with exceptions including Popcorn and Mushrooms. Food left on a Crock Pot or Drying Rack continues to spoil; Spoiled Eggs become Rotten Eggs."
  - "Environment and storage modify spoilage: food on the ground spoils 1.5× faster than in inventory or a chest; temperatures below 0 slow spoilage to 0.75× and above 70 increase it to 1.25×; wet food spoils at 1.3×. Ocean Fish can regain freshness in a Pinchin' Winch."
  - "Raw and cooked foods may be eaten directly; fires cook most raw food, Drying Racks turn eligible foods into long-lasting dried items, and Crock Pots produce dishes with additional benefits. Meat can bait carnivores, recruit Pigs, and be traded to Pig King; meat given to caged birds can produce Eggs. Carrots can befriend Bunnymen, but carrying Meat makes them hostile. Fruits and Vegetables bait herbivores and can be fed to Pigs for Manure; Seeds bait Bird Traps."
  - "Foods with a temperature effect change perceived temperature by 40 degrees. Cold effects last until the food rots; hot Crock Pot foods cool over time and cool four times faster in Ice Boxes or Snow Chester. Insulated Pack, Chef Pouch, and Bundling Wrap preserve their temperature effect; Hot Cocoa and Apple Cider remain hot indefinitely."
  - "In DST, eating a character's favorite food grants up to 15 additional Hunger, scaled by freshness. Favorites are recorded on the individual character entities."
  - "Meat foods use a longer chewing animation; non-meat foods are eaten quickly. Some foods harm Health or Sanity, and food freshness also affects the amount restored."
