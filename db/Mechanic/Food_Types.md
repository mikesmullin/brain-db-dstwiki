  description: "A food item's type is its basic diet classification and determines which characters and mobs can eat it. This is separate from Crock Pot food value, which controls recipe eligibility."
  fields:
  - "Main types include Veggie, Berry, Seeds, Meat, Monster, Goodies, Generic, Horrible, Roughage, Wood, Elementals, Nitre, Burnt, Raw, Gears, Lunar Shards, Miasma, and Carcass."
  - "Berry is a Veggie subtype: creatures that eat Veggies can also eat Berries, but Berry eaters need not eat other Veggies. Smallbirds and Sweetish Fish eat Berries."
  - "Monster is a Meat subtype: creatures that eat Meat can also eat Monster food, but Monster eaters need not eat other Meat. Wigfrid eats Meat and Wurt cannot; Chum is classified as Meat."
  - "Bunnymen favor Veggies, birds favor Seeds, and Pigs, Spiders, Hounds, and Wigfrid favor Meat; Wurt refuses Meat. Goodies can be eaten by Wigfrid. Horrible food suits Pigs, Hounds, and Spiders. Roughage is used to domesticate Beefalo."
  - "Only Cookie Cutters can eat Wood. Elementals are common food for Cave dwellers. WX-78 can consume Gears. Nitre is a subtype of Elementals, with the same one-way diet inheritance."
  - Lunar Shards suit Bright-Beaked Birds; Miasma suits Mega Blights; Carcass suits Hounds and Crystal-Crested Buzzards.
