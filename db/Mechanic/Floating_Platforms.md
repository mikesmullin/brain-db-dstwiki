  description: "Round structures that travel across the Ocean while carrying players, mobs, plants, and other structures. Boat variants range from 30 to 400 durability and support 3–8 structures; collisions with Sea Stacks, Salt Formations, coasts, or the world border can damage them and create leaks."
  fields:
  - "When durability reaches zero, the platform sinks: structures and plants aboard are destroyed, while players and non-flying, non-aquatic mobs drown and reappear at the nearest coast. Player-made Boats have a plank that lets players abandon ship with the same effects."
  - "Small leaks deal 0.5 damage per second and large leaks 1 damage per second. Bumpers reduce collision damage and leaks; a Captain's Tricorn halves collision damage and persistent durability loss."
  - "Repair values: Boards 50, Living Log 37.5, Boat Patch 25 and Tree Jam 20 for leaks, Twigs 13, Log or Driftwood Piece 12.5. Kelp Patch temporarily seals leaks for 4–6 minutes; Trusty Tape seals leaks. Tree Jam, Kelp Patch, and Trusty Tape only repair leaks."
  - "Move platforms with an Oar, Driftwood Oar, Claw Oar, or Malbatross Bill; Masts provide thrust and a Steering Wheel Kit directs them. An Anchor Kit stops or slows movement. Bootleg Getaway can create a Whirlportal to a revealed Ocean location."
  - "Below 15% Sanity while aboard a platform, Terrorclaws replace Terrorbeaks and Crawling Horrors. Below 75% Sanity, Wavey Jones appears on the Ocean in place of Night Hands."
  - "Platform variants include Archaic Boat (400 durability, 8 structures, 16-slot container), Boat and Pirate Sloop (200, 8), Dragonfly Boat (200, 4), Grass Raft (100, 8; sinks at 0.2 durability per second), Marotter Den (100, 8; sinks at 1 per second outside coastal waters), and Ice Boat (30, 8; fireproof, sinks at 2 per second, cannot carry structures)."
