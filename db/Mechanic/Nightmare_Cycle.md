  description: "A magical cycle in the Ruins and some Cave areas, independent of surface time, moon phase, and seasons. DST phases last: Calm 4.75–5.75 minutes, Warning 1–1.5 minutes, Nightmare 2.5–3.5 minutes, and Dawn 15–45 seconds (7.5–11.5 minutes total). The Ancient Gateway locks it at the peak Nightmare phase while the Ancient Key is inserted; removing the key or defeating the Ancient Fuelweaver ends the lock."
  fields:
  - "Calm: Nightmare Lights and Fissures close; Runic Turf is blue."
  - "Warning: lights and fissures open slightly but do not spawn Shadow Creatures; Runic Turf turns gray and Ancient Statues begin glowing."
  - "Nightmare: the three stages are Waxing, Steady, and Waning. Lights and fissures fully open and spawn Shadow Creatures; Runic Turf cracks glow red, Ancient Statues distort, and Splumonkeys become hostile Shadow Splumonkeys."
  - "Dawn: lights and fissures remain partly open but spawn no new Shadow Creatures; previously spawned creatures can persist for part of the phase. Splumonkeys remain shadow forms until Dawn ends and Calm begins."
  - A Thulecite Medallion changes color to show the current phase. Nightmare Lights and Ruins fissures synchronize with the cycle; Cave fissures can open and close independently.
